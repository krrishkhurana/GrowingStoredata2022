Growing STORE Data Analysis Project
Overview

Growing STORE is a leading retail company specializing in fashion and lifestyle products. This project focuses on analyzing business performance using data-driven techniques to evaluate financial health, operational efficiency, customer behavior, market positioning, and sustainability initiatives. The analysis aims to generate actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and business growth.

Project Objectives
Financial Analysis
Evaluate revenue, expenses, and profitability trends.
Analyze financial ratios such as:
Gross Profit Margin
Net Profit Margin
Current Ratio
Quick Ratio
Debt-to-Equity Ratio
Assess overall financial stability and performance.
Operational Analysis
Measure operational efficiency using key metrics:
Inventory Turnover
Asset Turnover
Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
Identify opportunities for process improvement and cost optimization.
Market Analysis
Examine industry trends and competitive positioning.
Analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
Identify growth opportunities and market expansion strategies.
Customer Analysis
Evaluate customer satisfaction and engagement.
Analyze retention rates and feedback data.
Understand customer preferences and buying behavior.
Sustainability & CSR Analysis
Review sustainability initiatives and environmental impact.
Assess ethical supply chain practices.
Analyze Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) efforts and their influence on brand reputation.
ETL Process

The project follows a structured ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) pipeline:

Extract
Collected retail sales and business data from source files.
Transform
Removed duplicate and irrelevant records.
Handled missing values and data inconsistencies.
Converted data types and standardized formats.
Performed outlier detection and data validation.
Load
Loaded cleaned and transformed data into the analytical environment for reporting and visualization.
